# ADR-241: Ashoka scoped amendment to ADR-096: exploration floors do not create factual evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-01T07:17:19.773Z<br>
**Status:** accepted<br>
**Decided by:** system<br>
**Affected entities:** src/cognitive/normalizer.ts, src/cognitive/evidence-ledger.ts, src/cognitive/trust-state.ts, src/cognitive/calibration-evaluation.ts, src/cognitive/engine.ts, ADR-096<br>

## Context

Source audit of normalizer.ts shows model semantic agreement, domain/keyword overlap and repeated reinforcement counted as independent promotion evidence, including a lowered cold-start evidence_count. Slice9 retained an actual unsupported promotion fixture. The owner-authorized unified Ashoka Slice15 requires independence and reviewed amendment before activation.

**Constraints:**
- Preserve ADR-096 entity-count OR cycle/time dual exit, confidence exploration ceiling 0.50, maximum 24h window and populated exit_reason.
- Old full-scan time alone does not imply stale evidence. Only known dirty scope, withdrawal, contradiction or changed source proof invalidates a claim.
- Keep source observations, explicit human assertions, hypotheses and validated insights distinguishable; preserve legacy evidence and evaluation history.

## Decision

Scoped system review under the human owner's already-approved Ashoka revision7/Slice15 authorizes replacing only ADR-096's relaxed factual-evidence clause. Cold start relaxes exploration/retention and confidence usability; factual promotion uses the same claim-specific independent source/observation ancestry checks in cold and mature graphs. Model agreement, repeated generation, endpoint existence and graph circulation may affect plausibility but never create independent factual corroboration. Current proof and typed endpoints are checked again at one durable promotion publication boundary. Unsupported legacy promotions remain inspectable and are re-evaluated; no live rewrite is authorized by this decision.

### Alternatives Considered

- **Keep the relaxed evidence count and add more model consensus** — Rejected: It perpetuates unsupported self-corroboration and fails the retained negative fixture.
- **Remove cold start entirely** — Rejected: It would unnecessarily discard the original usability policy and violate its dual exit guard.

## Consequences

- Cold-start exploration remains usable without manufacturing evidence.
- Claim ancestry, current proof, contradictions and operational versus labeled accuracy become inspectable.
- Real positive, repeated-ancestry, withdrawal and interruption fixtures must qualify the change before Slice15 acceptance.

**Risks accepted:**
- Legacy unsupported promotions will lose current validated applicability; history must survive.
- Producer and full consumer adoption must remain explicit and qualified, including Slices17/25/27.

## Guard Rails

> ⚠️ Do not remove or extend ADR-096's dual exit/window rules without separate review.

> ⚠️ Do not count semantic/model agreement or repeated reinforcement as independent evidence.

> ⚠️ Do not silently relabel human assertions or source hypotheses as facts.

> ⚠️ Do not mark Slice15 verified without supported positive evidence, independent-ancestry/cold-start/contradiction and durable-publication fixtures.

> ⚠️ No live migration or graph rewrite follows automatically from this scoped policy amendment.
